About

Ourenj Studio started in 2023 with a simple goal: to create an additional source of income while pursuing a passion for beauty.

Before starting the business, Mrs. Fara was working a regular 9-to-5 job and had no professional experience in nail services. Determined to learn, she joined a nail course and began building her skills from the ground up.

What started as a side income gradually grew into a business built around creativity, dedication, and a genuine love for the craft.

Today, Ourenj Studio is independently run by Kak Fara, who personally focuses on each client's experience.

The name Ourenj Studio was inspired by the phrase “Our Renjana.” Renjana represents passion and love — reflecting the heart behind the studio.

Beauty made personal, with passion.
