Meet Fara.

Every beautiful set begins with a little passion.

Ourenj Studio started in 2023 with a simple goal: to create an additional source of income while pursuing a passion for beauty.

Before starting the business, Fara worked a regular 9-to-5 job and had no professional experience in nail services. Determined to learn, she joined a nail course and began building her skills from the ground up.

Today, she independently runs Ourenj Studio, personally focusing on each client's experience.

Beauty made personal, with passion.
